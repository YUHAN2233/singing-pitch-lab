FFmpeg executable in this application is obtained from the pinned imageio-ffmpeg
0.6.0 platform wheel on PyPI. The package project and its wheel-building sources:
https://github.com/imageio/imageio-ffmpeg/tree/v0.6.0

FFmpeg source releases and licensing information:
https://ffmpeg.org/download.html#get-sources
https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html

The precise executable version and its configure flags are recorded in
FFMPEG_BUILD.txt generated during the macOS build. The application also includes
GPL-3.0.txt. Downstream redistributors should verify the wheel's corresponding
source and any non-FFmpeg linked libraries before further redistribution.
